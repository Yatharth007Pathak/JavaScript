What is "Towers of Hanio"?

Towers of Hanoi (also called Towers of Brahma or Lucas' Towers) is a mathematical puzzel. It was invented by a French mathematician in 1883. It consists of three rods, and a number of disks of different sizes which can slide onto any rod. The puzzle starts with the disks in a neat stack in ascending order of size on one rod, the smallest at the top.

What is the goal of the game?

The objective of the puzzle is to move the entire stack to another rod, obeying the following simple rules:

  • Only one disk can be moved at a time.
  • No disk may be placed on top of a smaller disk.
  • Only the discs on top of one towers can be moved to the top of other towers.
